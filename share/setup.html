Set up your own Gleanings

A pocket notebook for stories, illustrations, quotes, meetings and follow-ups. About 15 minutes, once, on a computer.

Before you start

  • It's yours alone. Gleanings runs in your own Google account. Your notes go to a folder in your own Drive. Nobody else can see them, including the person who shared this with you.
  • You need: a Google (Gmail) account, a computer for this setup, and your phone.
  • AI is optional. Without it, you pick the type of each note yourself. With a key (step 8), Gleanings sorts and tidies notes and reads photos of pages for you.

1Create your script

  1. On your computer, go to script.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click New project. At the top, click Untitled project and rename it Gleanings.

2Paste the code

  1. Open the code: Code.gs. Select everything (Ctrl+A, or ⌘A on a Mac) and copy it.
  2. Back in your script, click into Code.gs, select everything there, delete it, and paste. Click the Save icon.
  3. Click the gear (Project Settings) on the left and tick Show "appsscript.json" manifest file in editor.
  4. Go back to the editor (the < > icon) and open appsscript.json. Open appsscript.json, copy all of it, replace everything in that file, and Save.

3Run firstSetup

  1. In the toolbar, choose firstSetup from the function menu and click Run.
  2. Click Review permissions and choose your account.
  3. Google will say "Google hasn't verified this app." That is normal: it's your own script, which only you use. Click Advanced, then Go to Gleanings (unsafe), then Allow.

Gleanings asks to use your Drive, Docs, Sheets and Calendar so it can file your notes and add follow-ups. It runs only inside your own account.

4Copy your setup code

  1. When it finishes, the Execution log opens at the bottom. (If not, click Execution log.)
  2. It shows Your one-time SETUP CODE, like ABCD-2345. Write it down. It works for 48 hours.
  3. It also shows a link to your new Gleanings folder in Drive, with your own Master Resources, Meeting Notes and logs.

5Deploy it

  1. Click Deploy → New deployment.
  2. Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose Web app.
  3. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then click Deploy.
  4. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec). This is your server link.
Why "Anyone"? So your phone app can reach it. Without your password, it opens nothing: every request is checked, and after 10 wrong tries it locks for 15 minutes.

6Open it on your phone and choose your password

Open Gleanings

  1. Open that link on your phone (send it to yourself by text or email).
  2. Paste your server link and tap Next.
  3. On the welcome screen, enter your setup code and your first name, then choose your own password (at least 8 characters). Tap Set up my Gleanings.
Write your password down somewhere safe. If you ever forget it, open your script, choose resetPassword and click Run. Your notes are kept, and you get a new setup code to choose a new password.

7Put it on your home screen

  • Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ → Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

It opens full-screen like any app. Updates arrive on their own.

8Optional: turn on AI

In Gleanings, tap the gear (Settings) and paste one key:

  • Claude: create a key at console.anthropic.com → Settings → API keys (it starts with sk-ant-api03-). Add a little billing credit first. Light use usually costs cents a day.
  • Gemini: create a key at aistudio.google.com → Get API key. There is a free level. Google may use what you send on the free level to improve its products, so check its terms first.
Never paste a key or your password into a chat, an email or a document. It goes only into Gleanings' Settings. If a key ever gets out, delete it on the site where you made it and create a new one. Be careful in the account settings there: deleting a key is different from deleting the whole account.

Keeping it safe

  • Don't give anyone your server link and password together. That would open your notes.
  • If your phone is lost, run resetPassword in your script. The old password stops working at once.
  • Patient and counseling privacy: keep names, rooms, diagnoses and other identifying details out of notes. Mark sensitive notes Private: private notes stay in your script's private settings and never go to Drive.

Gleanings is shared freely by a friend. The code is open at github.com/santoshsarah09-hub/gleanings-app; your notes are never stored there.
